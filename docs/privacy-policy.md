# Tarry privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

Tarry is an Android app made by Kori Jacobsen in Australia ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: **your data stays on your phone.** Tarry has no account, no server and no analytics.

## What Tarry records
During the work hours you set, Tarry records your phone's location (and, if you allow it, whether you are walking,
driving or still). It uses these to draw your day as site visits and trips. You can add jobs, notes and place names.

All of this is stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage. We never receive it. It is not backed up to
Google's cloud by the app.

## What leaves your phone, and why
Tarry only connects to the internet for three things:

1. **Map tiles** — when you open a map, the app downloads map images for the area on screen from OpenFreeMap
   (openfreemap.org). Like any website, that server sees your IP address and which map area was requested. No account or
   identifier is sent.
2. **Street addresses** — to name a place ("12 Smith St"), the app asks your phone's built-in Android geocoder, which may
   send the place's coordinates to Google to look up the address. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.
3. **Your purchase** — the 7-day trial runs on your phone. If you buy Tarry, Google Play handles the payment and tells the
   app whether you own it. We receive no payment details and send none of your app data to Google Play.

Nothing else is sent. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no trackers.

## Exports and backups
You can export CSV/PDF reports and full backups. These files go only where you choose to save or send them. Once you
share a file, the place you send it to handles it under its own terms.

## Your control
- Change or pause tracking at any time; it only runs during your hours or when you start it.
- Delete individual records, or everything at once: Settings → Data → Delete all data.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.
- Location records older than your retention setting are removed automatically.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, hand over or delete on a server.

## Children
Tarry is for adults managing their work hours and is not directed at children.
