## Full description (≤ 4000)

Tarry quietly notes where you are during work hours, splits your day into on-site time and travel, and lets you drop jobs onto it later. No account. No cloud. It all stays on your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
• Paint your hours once. Tell Tarry when you work. It starts and stops on its own from then on.
• Work like normal. Tarry stays in the background, noting each site you stop at and each drive between.
• Drop jobs on at the end of the day. See your day drawn to scale, add a job across the visits and trips it covers, and your hours are ready to invoice.

FEATURES
• A day you can read at a glance: visits and trips drawn to scale like a calendar, with jobs alongside.
• Jobs that span the real work: one job can cover a morning of site visits and the drive between.
• Suggests, never assumes: set a usual job for a place and Tarry suggests it. You accept or decline.
• Never fails silently: if Android stops tracking, or a permission is missing, Tarry tells you and marks the gap. It never makes up the missing time.
• Map and scrubber: replay the day on a map to remember where you were at 2:40.
• Summary and export: totals by job with your rounding and minimums, plus CSV and PDF for your invoices.
• Your backup, your file: export a full backup to wherever you choose.
• Battery modes: Balanced by default, Precise when you want every detail, Saver for long days, and an automatic switch to Saver at low battery.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT. NOT BY SETTING.
Tarry has no account, no server and no analytics. Your location is processed on your phone and never leaves it, unless you export a file yourself. The only things it fetches from the internet are map tiles and street addresses (the phone's built-in geocoder), and Google Play handles your purchase.
• No sign-up, no email, no tracking pixels.
• Location stays on your device.
• You choose where backups and exports go.
• Delete everything in one tap.

ONE PRICE. NO SUBSCRIPTION.
Free for 7 days with everything included. Then one payment, yours for life on your Google account: unlimited tracking and history; jobs, summary and exports; backups and restore; all future updates to the core app. After the trial your data stays yours: you can still view, edit, export, back up and delete everything.

WHY TARRY NEEDS LOCATION "ALL THE TIME"
Work happens with your phone in your pocket. Tarry records your location in the background only during the hours you set (or when you start it yourself), shows a notification while it does, and keeps everything on your phone.
