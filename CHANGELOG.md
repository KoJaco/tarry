# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (versionCode 2)
- The "Tarry is tracking" notification now appears as soon as notifications are allowed. Before, tracking that started
  during onboarding (before the notifications step) ran with no visible notification until it restarted.
- Privacy wording: the app, store listing and policy now say exactly what leaves the phone (place positions to the
  phone's geocoder, the viewed map area to OpenFreeMap).

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1)
First Play release: automatic work-hours timeline (visits and trips), jobs, place suggestions, map and day scrubber,
summary with rounding, CSV and PDF export, backup and restore, tracking health and gap reporting, battery modes
(Precise, Balanced, Saver, low-battery switch), 7-day free trial and lifetime unlock through Google Play.
